FAQ

Is Freedom Hat really free?

Yes. Free to download, free to use, free to share. Forever. The project is funded by community donations.

Can I try it without installing?

Absolutely. Boot from the USB stick into Live Mode and test-drive everything without touching your hard drive.

Can I keep Windows or macOS alongside it?

Yep. Independence Installer can set up dual-boot automatically, so you pick your OS every time you start up.

Will my apps work?

Most popular apps — browsers, Spotify, Discord, Steam, VS Code, LibreOffice — are one click away in the Frontier App Store.

How long is 1.0 “Patriotism” supported?

Patriotism receives security updates for 5 years, through July 2031.
