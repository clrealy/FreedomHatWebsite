FAQ

What is FHCL based on?

FHCL is Debian-based (built on Kubuntu) and ships the KDE Plasma desktop, debloated before anything else is installed.

Why does apt say it’s disabled?

On FHCL, software goes through eagle. Use eagle install, eagle remove, eagle upgrade, eagle search and eagle clean. It uses the same Debian packages under the hood.

What does the first-boot picker install?

Office (LibreOffice), Media Codecs, Gaming (Steam, Lutris, Wine), Dev Tools and Extra KDE Apps. Check what you want and skip the rest. It runs once per user.

What’s the liberty command?

A built-in toolkit: liberty info for specs, liberty boot for boot-time history, liberty clean to free up space, and liberty update to update the system and Flatpaks together. Try liberty salute too. 🦅

Can I get Flatpak apps?

Yes. Flathub is enabled out of the box, along with the universe and multiverse repos.

Is it free?

Yes. Free to download, free to use, free to share.
